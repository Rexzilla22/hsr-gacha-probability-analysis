# Simulation Results

## 1. Overview

The project compares theoretical probability calculations with Monte Carlo simulations for three saving scenarios in Honkai: Star Rail.

The simulation uses 100,000 simulated players.

## 2. Results

| Scenario | Pulls | Theoretical Probability | Simulated Probability |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| F2P | 72 | 35.1635% | 35.146% |
| Monthly Pass | 88 | 99.8187% | 99.799% |
| Monthly Pass + BP | 97 | 100% | 100% |

These probabilities describe the chance of receiving at least one 5-star character, not necessarily the featured character.

## 3. Interpretation

- The F2P scenario has a 35.16% theoretical chance of receiving at least one 5-star within 72 pulls.
- The Monthly Pass scenario reaches approximately 99.82% within 88 pulls.
- The Monthly Pass + Battle Pass scenario reaches 100% within 97 pulls under the simplified model.

The results show how additional pulls and the pity system increase the probability of receiving a 5-star character.

## 4. Simulation Validation

The simulated probabilities are very close to the theoretical calculations.

The largest absolute difference is 0.000197, equivalent to 0.0197 percentage points.

This indicates that the Monte Carlo simulation is consistent with the theoretical model.

## 5. Important Limitation

A 5-star result does not necessarily mean that the featured character is obtained. The 50/50 mechanic and featured character guarantee must be considered separately.
